    What the analysis says

      Full analysis

      Boost held

      Average boost in the tank (0–1). Recorded from ~1.4B steps.

      Time at zero boost

      Share of the time a car has no boost at all. Recorded from ~1.4B steps.

      Boost collected

      Boost picked up per car per minute (a full tank is 100).

      Ball speed

      Average ball speed (uu/s). Rises as hits get harder.

      Car speed

      Average car speed (uu/s). Supersonic is 2200. Recorded from ~1.4B steps.

      Time in the air

      Share of the time a car is airborne. Recorded from ~1.4B steps.

      Ball touches

      Touches per car per minute. More is not always better (ball hugging).

      Aerial touches

      Share of touches made in the air with the ball above ~250 uu.

      Demolitions

      Demos per simulated match minute.

      Win rate vs the 400M version

      A fixed yardstick for long-term progress: JakeBot at 400M steps. 1v1, mixed start situations, first goal wins, draws excluded. 50% = no better.

      Win rate vs recent versions

      Against versions 50–250M steps older (recent progress). 1v1, mixed starts. 50% = no better.

      Latest tests

      Head-to-head games in RocketSim between two JakeBot versions (always the most likely action, sides swapped). Run during the hourly check-ins at low CPU priority. A single 200-game test can be off by several percent: trust trends and 400-game tests.

      Past-version opponents (training)

      Policy entropy

      Randomness of the policy. Max 4.50.

      Average return

      Average discounted return the critic is trained on. A training signal, not skill; jumps whenever reward weights change.

      Value function loss

      How well the critic predicts returns. Spikes after reward changes are normal.

      KL divergence

      How much the policy changes per update. Should stay small (≈0.001–0.01).

      Clip fraction

      Share of samples hitting the PPO clip. Healthy ≈ 0.01–0.1.

      Collection speed

      Steps/s gathered by the simulation (learning runs alongside).

      Current reward weights

      What the bot is paid for right now (relative sizes matter).

      Curriculum (spawn mix)

      Curriculum phases

      The run moves through these phases by total steps; each switch restarts training automatically. ETA assumes the current speed.

      Reward-change history

      Checkpoints (newest 3 kept)

      Service log

      Training starts, stops and automatic restarts.
      Published automatically from the training PC every ~5 minutes while it is on. If the status says “stale”, the PC is off, asleep, or training was stopped.
